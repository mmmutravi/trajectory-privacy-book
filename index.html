01 / ABOUT THE BOOK

Making mobility data
useful and private.

The edited volume explores the balance between data utility and privacy in pervasive, mobile, and location-aware environments. It brings together research on trajectory data, location-based services (LBS), mobility analytics, privacy threats, re-identification risks, and trustworthy processing of spatiotemporal information.

The book addresses privacy models, privacy-preserving techniques, secure trajectory processing, and intelligent approaches that enable meaningful analysis while protecting sensitive movement patterns. It also examines AI, machine learning, IoT, blockchain, and distributed technologies for secure location-based systems, with applications in smart cities, intelligent transportation, healthcare, emergency services, tourism, and logistics.

05 / FOR CONTRIBUTORS

Submission guidelines

Prepare a focused proposal that clearly explains the research contribution and its relevance to the volume.

A

Proposal requirements

  • Submit a chapter proposal / extended abstract of 500–800 words.
  • Outline the objectives, scope, approach, and contributions.
  • Explain the problem, research objectives, proposed method or review approach, and expected contributions.
  • Describe how the chapter advances privacy-preserving trajectory intelligence or trustworthy location-based systems.
B

Types of contributions

  • Theoretical foundations and novel algorithms.
  • System architectures and comparative studies.
  • Experimental evaluations and implementation reports.
  • Case studies and comprehensive surveys.
  • Application-oriented research and technical frameworks.
C

Important notes

  • Submissions must be original and not under consideration elsewhere.
  • Authors must comply with publication ethics.
  • Permission is required for third-party figures, tables, datasets, and copyrighted material.
  • Publisher requirements concerning review, similarity screening, AI-assisted tools, charges, royalties, and contributor agreements will apply.
